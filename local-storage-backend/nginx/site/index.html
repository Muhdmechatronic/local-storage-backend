Features

Everything you need to build healthier habits

One app for moving more, eating better and seeing real progress, designed to be simple enough to use every day.

GPS workout tracking

Record walks, runs and rides with live distance, time, speed and pace. Your route is colour-coded by speed, and tracking keeps going with the screen locked.

AI meal scanning

Take a photo of your meal and get an estimated breakdown of calories and nutrients in seconds. Prefer typing? Add foods manually instead.

Step counting

Count your daily steps with your phone's motion sensor and review your activity history over time.

Before vs After progress

Compare your weight and BMI with your starting point, follow the trend on a chart with your target marked, and review every update.

Personal calorie target

Get a daily calorie goal calculated from your profile and activity level, whether you want to lose, maintain or gain weight.

Daily challenges

Stay motivated with small daily challenges that keep you moving and make healthy habits stick.

How it works

Start in three simple steps

Create your profile

Sign up and enter your age, height, weight and goal. Your first measurement becomes your baseline.

Track and log

Record workouts, count your steps and log meals with a photo or by hand as you go through your day.

See your progress

Watch your trends, compare before and after, and adjust your goals as you improve.

Privacy first

Your health data stays under your control

We collect only what the app needs to work, explain exactly how it is used, and let you remove it whenever you choose.

Location only during workouts

GPS is used only while you record a workout you started, with a notification always visible.

No ads, no selling

We do not show advertising and we never sell your personal data.

Encrypted connections

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with Google Firebase, encrypted at rest.

Delete any time

Remove individual records in the app, or request full account deletion.

About Beyond Healthy

Beyond Healthy is developed by the Beyond Healthy Team as part of a university research project on everyday physical activity, nutrition and wellbeing. The app helps participants and everyday users build healthier routines, while giving the research team the insight they need to support them.

Beyond Healthy is a general fitness and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
