Delete your Beyond Healthy account

You can ask us to permanently delete your Beyond Healthy account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, what is removed and how long it takes.

App developer: Beyond Healthy Team Completed within 30 days

01How to request deletion

  1. Email us from your registered address

    Send an email to beyondhealthy10@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Beyond Healthy, so we can confirm you own the account.

  2. Use the subject "Delete my account"

    No other details are required. You may tell us if you only want specific data removed instead.

  3. We confirm and delete

    We reply to confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a final confirmation once it is done.

Send your request to

beyondhealthy10@gmail.com
Request deletion

02What happens to your data

Permanently deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in details
  • Your health profile: age, gender, occupation, height, weight, target weight, BMI and calorie goals
  • Weight and BMI history
  • Workout records, including routes and location data
  • Step and activity logs
  • Food logs and meal photos
  • Challenge progress and wellbeing records

May be kept

  • Information we are required to keep by law, and only for as long as the law requires
  • Research exports made before your request, handled under the university's research data retention rules and never published in a form that identifies you

03Delete specific data without closing your account

You can delete individual food logs and activity logs directly in the App at any time. To remove other specific data while keeping your account, email beyondhealthy10@gmail.com and tell us what you would like removed.

For more about how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.
