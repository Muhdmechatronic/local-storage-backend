01Summary

An account is required. We collect your name and email address.

We store the health and fitness data you enter or record, such as weight, workouts, steps and meals.

Precise location is used only while you record a workout or use a map feature.

Authorised researchers from the university research project can view and export your records.

We do not sell your data, show advertising or use advertising or analytics trackers.

You can correct your data in the app and request deletion of your account at any time.

02Who we are

Beyond Healthy (the "App", Android package com.isbeyond.healthy) is developed and operated by the Beyond Healthy Team ("we", "us", "our") as part of a university research project on physical activity, nutrition and wellbeing. We are responsible for the personal data processed through the App.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of the Google Play Developer Program Policies and the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (PDPA). By creating an account and accepting this policy in the App, you consent to the processing described here.

03Personal identification data

Yes, the App collects personal identification data. Specifically:

  • Name and email address, which you provide when you register.
  • Password, which is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.
  • Google account basic profile (name, email address and account identifier), only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, messages, call logs, advertising identifier or government identification numbers.

04Information we collect

Profile information you provide

Age, gender, occupation, activity level, height, weight, target weight and calorie goal preferences. The App calculates your BMI and daily calorie target from this information.

Health and fitness information

  • Workout records: activity type, duration, distance, speed, pace, cadence, elevation and route.
  • Step counts, recorded with your device's motion sensor (Physical Activity permission).
  • Weight and BMI history, recorded each time you update your profile.
  • Food logs: meals, calorie and nutrient estimates, and meal photos.
  • Daily challenge progress.
  • Mental wellbeing records, such as stress level, where these are recorded for your account.

Location information

Precise location (GPS), collected only while you record a workout or use a map feature in the App. See Location permissions.

Photos and camera

When you take a photo of a meal or choose one from your gallery, only that photo is uploaded for analysis and stored with your food log. The App does not access any other photos on your device.

Information stored on your device

The App keeps your settings and a temporary image cache on your device to improve performance. Cached images expire after 14 days and are removed when you uninstall the App.

05Location permissions

Why we need precise location

The App requests precise location because accurate fitness tracking depends on it. Approximate location cannot measure the distance, speed or route of a walk, run or ride with meaningful accuracy.

Location while the screen is locked

Most workouts happen with the phone in a pocket and the screen off. To record your full route, the App continues to collect location while the screen is locked or while you use another app, but only during a workout that you have started yourself.

  • Tracking runs as a foreground service, and a persistent notification is shown for as long as it is active.
  • Tracking stops as soon as you end the workout.
  • The App does not collect location when no workout is being recorded and no map feature is open.
  • Location data is used only to calculate and display your route, distance, speed and pace. It is never used for advertising and is never sold.

You can change or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings > Apps > Beyond Healthy > Permissions. Without it, GPS workout tracking will not work, but the rest of the App remains available.

06How we use information

We use your information to:

  • Create, secure and maintain your account.
  • Record, display and summarise your workouts, steps, meals and progress.
  • Calculate your BMI, calorie targets and progress comparisons.
  • Estimate the nutritional content of meal photos you submit.
  • Provide daily challenges.
  • Allow the research team to support participants and to analyse and report on the research project (see Sharing and third parties).
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

The research team

Beyond Healthy is part of a university research project. Authorised administrators from the research team can view user profiles and records, including health, fitness, food and progress data, and can export this data for participant support, analysis and reporting within the project. Administrators may use the data only for these purposes. Results that are published or presented will be reported in a form that does not identify you.

Other disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law or by a valid request from a public authority, to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others, or in connection with a transfer of the App to another operator, in which case you will be notified.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

08International transfers

Firebase and other Google services may store and process your data on servers outside Malaysia. Google applies its own security and data protection safeguards to this processing. Meal photos are stored on our own server in Malaysia. By using the App, you consent to these transfers as permitted under the PDPA.

09Data security

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Data stored in Firebase is encrypted at rest by Google.
  • Requests to our server must carry a valid sign-in token, so only signed-in users can upload or analyse photos.
  • Access to the administrator area is limited to authorised members of the research team.
  • Meal photos are stored on our server and can be opened through a direct link. We do not publish these links.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

10Data retention

  • Account, profile, health and fitness data are kept for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account and associated data, including meal photos, within 30 days, unless we are required by law to keep it.
  • Exports made by the research team before your deletion request are handled under the university's research data retention rules.
  • Cached images on your device expire after 14 days and are removed when you uninstall the App.

11Your rights and choices

Under the PDPA and Google Play policy, you can:

  • Access and correct your profile information at any time in the App under Health Profile.
  • Delete individual records, such as food logs and activity logs, directly in the App.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by following the steps on our account deletion page, or by emailing beyondhealthy10@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw your consent or ask us to limit how we process your data. Withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use some or all of the App.
  • Withdraw permissions such as location, camera, physical activity and notifications in your device settings at any time.

We respond to access and correction requests within 21 days, and to other requests within 30 days.

12Health disclaimer

Beyond Healthy is a general fitness and wellness app. It is not a medical device, and its calorie, nutrient, BMI and activity estimates are not medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.

13Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Users under 18 should use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. When we make significant changes, the App will ask you to review and accept the updated policy before you continue.
